Better Than Mountains Are Only Mountains…

https://youtu.be/EItliOEryb8?si=HLd7M8wOrM42iSrs

The evening fell… Long shadows drifted down the height, 
The crimson apple of the sun burned to its end. 
Through leaves the mist came pouring, soft and silver-white, 
And wrapped the waiting earth with tenderness to lend.

Each passing day leaves footprints on the road we tread, 
While night’s dark mantle calls the soul to something real. 
Whispering pines, and listening oaks o’erhead, 
Compose a forest symphony with fearless zeal.

Darkness blooms as fireflies awaken into flight, 
As though a hidden spark could set the flesh to sing. 
A lone cicada breaks the silence of the night, 
Its piercing cry can touch the deepest hidden string.

The midnight rain drums softly on the weathered roof. 
By dawn, whatever ripened opens into bloom. 
The rising sun ascends as morning’s living proof, 
When mind grows still, pure witnessing dispels the gloom.
